# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 10, 2026

## Overview
LocalLink is designed from the ground up with a strict "Zero Cloud Footprint" philosophy. We believe your data belongs solely to you.

## Data Collection and Usage
- **No Cloud Servers:** LocalLink does not use, connect to, or transmit data to any central servers, cloud databases, or third-party analytics services.
- **Local Storage Only:** All messages, files, cryptographic keys, and contact information are stored locally on your device in an encrypted format.
- **Peer-to-Peer:** Data transmission occurs directly between devices over your Local Area Network (LAN) or Wi-Fi network.

## Permissions
LocalLink requires the following permissions to function:
- **Camera:** Exclusively used to scan QR codes for secure, out-of-band cryptographic pairing. Images are processed locally and immediately discarded.
- **Network / Wi-Fi State:** Used to discover peers on the same local network via UDP Multicast.
- **Notifications:** Used to alert you when a new message is received while the app is in the background.

## Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the repository maintainer.
